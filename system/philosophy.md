# Philosophy

## First principles

The system starts from the question:

> What does a person actually need in order to communicate in this situation?

Not from:

> What should a French textbook teach?

## Core idea

Learning exists to increase what the user can **do** with the language:

1. **Produce** — express something
2. **Comprehend** — understand real French
3. **Interact** — participate in real situations
4. **Repair** — keep the interaction going when something goes wrong

## Garden metaphor

- The **garden** is the user's total communicative ability.
- A **seed** is a concrete capability the user wants to grow.
- A **sprint** plants or waters a seed.
- **Harvest** is the usable capability that results.

## Guiding belief

> Do not learn French as a collection of knowledge.  
> Learn French by continuously increasing what you can communicate, understand, interact with, and repair.
