# S003 — Ask to Repeat

## Seed

basic-repair (extension)

## Circumstance

Someone said something you didn’t catch (or that was too fast/unclear). You already know how to say you don’t understand. Now you need a simple, polite way to ask them to say it again.

## Communicative objective

Be able to politely ask someone to repeat what they just said.

## Core phrase

Pourriez-vous répéter, s'il vous plaît ?

## Meaning

Could you repeat (that), please?

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- Pourriez-vous — Could you (conditional of *pouvoir*, more polite)
- répéter — to repeat
- s'il vous plaît — please

### Structure

Pourriez-vous + infinitive + s'il vous plaît ?

### Grammar / pattern

The conditional *pourriez-vous* is a classic polite way to make a request in French. It softens the demand compared to the present *vous pouvez*.

### Pronunciation notes

- Pourriez-vous répéter, s'il vous plaît ?
  → /pu.ʁje vu ʁe.pe.te sil vu plɛ/

Smooth unit: **Pourriez-vous répéter, s'il vous plaît ?**

### Context

Very polite and appropriate in shops, cafés, with strangers, and in service situations. Excellent default for formal or careful speech.

## Variations

1. Pourriez-vous répéter, s'il vous plaît ? — Could you repeat, please? (core – preferred)
2. Vous pouvez répéter, s'il vous plaît ? — Can you repeat, please? (slightly more direct)
3. Pardon, pourriez-vous répéter ? — Sorry, could you repeat?
4. Encore une fois, s'il vous plaît. — One more time, please.
5. Répétez, s'il vous plaît. — Repeat, please. (more direct)

## Practice summary

Observed, broken down, pronounced, recalled, transformed (with variants), and used in situation. Preferred form set to the conditional.

## Proof of capability (completed)

User demonstrated:

- Correct recognition and meaning of “Pourriez-vous répéter, s'il vous plaît ?”
- Accurate production without looking
- Preference for the more polite conditional form
- Ability to use at least one short variation
- Ability to chain it with “Je ne comprends pas” or “Pardon ?”
- Appropriate use in a realistic situation

## Before

Could signal “I don’t understand” but had no clear way to request a repetition.

## After

Can say **Pourriez-vous répéter, s'il vous plaît ?** (and variants) and run a short repair chain.

## Status

growing

## Observations

- User prefers the conditional form “Pourriez-vous…” for higher politeness.
- Both “Pourriez-vous” and “Vous pouvez” are useful; the conditional is the safer, more polite default.
- Natural next extensions: ask someone to speak more slowly, or combine the full repair toolkit with requests.
