# S004 — Speak More Slowly

## Seed

basic-repair (extension)

## Circumstance

Someone is speaking too fast. You already know how to say you don’t understand and how to ask them to repeat. Now you need a clear way to ask them to slow down.

## Communicative objective

Be able to politely ask someone to speak more slowly.

## Core phrase

Pourriez-vous parler plus lentement, s'il vous plaît ?

## Meaning

Could you speak more slowly, please?

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- Pourriez-vous — Could you
- parler — to speak
- plus lentement — more slowly
- s'il vous plaît — please

### Structure

Pourriez-vous + infinitive + adverb + s'il vous plaît ?

### Grammar / pattern

Reuses the exact polite request frame from S003. Only the verb and adverb change.

### Pronunciation notes

- Pourriez-vous parler plus lentement, s'il vous plaît ?
  → /pu.ʁje vu paʁ.le ply lɑ̃t.mɑ̃ sil vu plɛ/

Smooth unit: **Pourriez-vous parler plus lentement, s'il vous plaît ?**

### Context

Polite and natural in any situation where someone is speaking too quickly (shops, cafés, conversations with strangers, etc.).

## Variations

1. Pourriez-vous parler plus lentement, s'il vous plaît ? — Could you speak more slowly, please? (core – preferred)
2. Vous pouvez parler plus lentement, s'il vous plaît ? — Can you speak more slowly, please?
3. Plus lentement, s'il vous plaît. — More slowly, please. (short)
4. Lentement, s'il vous plaît. — Slowly, please. (even shorter)

## Practice summary

Observed, broken down, pronounced, recalled, transformed (with shorter variants), and used in situation. Full repair chain practiced.

## Proof of capability (completed)

User demonstrated:

- Correct recognition and meaning of “Pourriez-vous parler plus lentement, s'il vous plaît ?”
- Accurate production without looking
- Ability to use at least one shorter variation
- Ability to chain it with previous repair phrases
- Appropriate use when someone is speaking too fast

## After
