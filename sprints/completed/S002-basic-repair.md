# S002 — Basic Repair

## Seed

basic-repair

## Circumstance

You are in a shop, café, or any real interaction. Someone says something you don’t understand (or speaks too fast). You need a simple, polite way to signal this so the conversation doesn’t break.

## Communicative objective

Be able to clearly and politely signal that you do not understand.

## Core phrase

Je ne comprends pas.

## Meaning

I don’t understand. / I don’t get it.

## Breakdown

### Vocabulary

- Je — I
- ne … pas — negation frame
- comprends — understand (1st person singular of *comprendre*)

### Structure

Je + ne + comprends + pas

### Grammar / pattern

This is the standard, neutral way to say you don’t understand. It works in almost any situation.

### Pronunciation notes

- Je ne comprends pas → /ʒə nə kɔ̃.pʁɑ̃ pa/
  (In natural speech the *ne* is often weakened or dropped: “Je comprends pas”)

Smooth unit: **Je ne comprends pas.**

### Context

Neutral and polite enough for shops, cafés, strangers, and service situations. Safe default.

## Variations

1. Je ne comprends pas. — I don’t understand. (core)
2. Pardon ? — Sorry? / Pardon? (very short, common for “what did you say?”)
3. Je ne comprends pas bien. — I don’t understand very well.
4. Désolé, je ne comprends pas. — Sorry, I don’t understand.

## Practice summary

Observed, broken down, pronounced, recalled, transformed (with short variants), and used in situation.

## Proof of capability (completed)

User demonstrated:

- Correct recognition and meaning of “Je ne comprends pas.”
- Accurate production without looking
- Ability to use at least one short variation (Pardon ? or Désolé, je ne comprends pas.)
- Appropriate use in a shop/café-style situation
- Ability to keep an interaction going when something is not understood

## Before

No simple way to signal that you don’t understand.

## After

Can say **Je ne comprends pas.** (and short variants such as Pardon ? or Désolé, je ne comprends pas.) to keep the interaction alive.
